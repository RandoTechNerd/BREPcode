title: CR2032 / coin cell
match: cr2032, cr2016, cr2025, coin cell, button cell, button battery, watch battery
---

Coin cells are named for their size: CR**20**32 = **20.0mm diameter**, **3.2mm**
thick. So CR2016 is 20.0 x 1.6mm and CR2025 is 20.0 x 2.5mm — same footprint,
different thickness. Design the diameter once and let thickness be a variable.

Pocket: **20.6mm bore, thickness + 0.3mm deep.** They are thin and stiff, so
clearance can be tighter than a cylindrical cell.

Add a **finger notch** — a 6-8mm scallop cut into the rim — or the user cannot
get the cell back out without a screwdriver. This is the single most common
mistake in a coin-cell holder.

**Keep out of reach of children**: a swallowed coin cell burns through tissue
within hours. If the design is a toy, a keepsake or anything a child handles,
say plainly that the battery compartment needs a screw, not a friction fit.

You have every dimension you need — build it.
