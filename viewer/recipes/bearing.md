Common bearings, **bore × outer Ø × width** (mm):

- 608 (skateboard, the default if unspecified): 8 × 22 × 7
- 625: 5 × 16 × 5 · 626: 6 × 19 × 6 · 623: 3 × 10 × 4
- 688: 8 × 16 × 5 (slim 608 bore)
- 6001: 12 × 28 × 8 · 6201: 12 × 32 × 10
- 6800: 10 × 19 × 5 · 6802: 15 × 24 × 5 (thin sections)

Printed fits, on DIAMETER:

- Press fit pocket: outer Ø **+0.1** (push in with a clamp, holds without glue)
- Slip fit: outer Ø **+0.3**
- Shaft through the bore: bore **−0.1** for press, bore +0.2 to spin freely
- Pocket depth = width **+0.2**; add a Ø(outer−3) through-hole behind it so the
  bearing can be pushed back out, and a 0.5mm lip in front if it must not walk.

Seat the bearing on its OUTER ring: a shoulder that touches the inner ring drags.
Shoulder/lip contact ≤ 1.5mm radial so it clears the inner ring.

You have every dimension you need — build it. State which bearing you assumed.
