Generic T-slot / V-slot profiles (2020 = 20×20mm and so on):

- **2020**: slot opening ≈ **6.2** wide, slot inner cavity ≈ 11-12 wide × 6
  deep, centre bore Ø4.2 (taps M5). Fasten with **M5 T-nuts** + M5 screws.
- 2040 / 2060 / 2080: same 6.2 slots on a 20mm pitch, more of them.
- **3030**: slot opening ≈ **8.2**, M6 or M8 T-nuts, centre bore Ø6.8 (M8).
- 4040: like 3030 but 40 square; some brands use the 6.2 slot ×2 — check.

Printed parts that mate with it:

- A tab that drops INTO a 2020 slot: **5.8 wide × 3.5 tall** (0.4 under the
  opening), any length along the slot. It locates; the T-nut clamps.
- Slide-on end cap / foot for 2020: inner pocket **20.4 × 20.4**, wall ≥ 2,
  8-10 deep; add four 1.5-wide ribs down the pocket corners for grip.
- Corner bracket: through-holes Ø5.5 (M5 clearance +) at **10mm** from the
  joint face — that centres them on the slot of a 2020.
- Screw straight into the centre bore end: M5 into the Ø4.2 bore (self-taps).

V-slot vs plain T-slot: V-slot's slot edges are bevelled 45° for wheels; the
opening and T-nuts are compatible with the numbers above for brackets/tabs.

You have every dimension you need — build it. Assume 2020/M5 unless told.
