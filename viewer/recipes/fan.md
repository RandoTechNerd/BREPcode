Square axial fans. Columns: **frame size · mounting-hole spacing (square
pattern, centre-to-centre) · typical depths**:

- 40mm: 32.0 · 10 or 20 deep
- 50mm: 40.0 · 10/15
- 60mm: 50.0 · 15/25
- 70mm: 61.5 · 15/25
- 80mm: 71.5 · 25
- 92mm: 82.5 · 25
- 120mm: 105.0 · 25 (the desktop default)
- 140mm: 124.5 · 25

Holes: Ø4.4 for standard self-tapping fan screws, or Ø3.4 + nuts for M3 (40mm
fans) / Ø4.5 for M4. Corner holes sit ON the square pattern above, centred in
the frame.

Airflow opening: circle Ø = frame − 3 (120mm fan → Ø117). A printed grill:
concentric rings 2 wide × 2 thick, gaps ≤ 7 for fingers, joined by 3-4 radial
spokes; or difference() a honeycomb. Leave ≥ 10mm clearance behind the intake
side or it just hisses.

Duct/shroud: inner Ø at the fan = frame − 3, wall 1.6-2.4, taper no steeper
than 30° per side.

You have every dimension you need — build it. 120mm × 25 unless told otherwise.
