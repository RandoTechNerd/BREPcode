A frame is a border with a **rabbet** — a step on the back that the picture and
glazing drop into.

- Rabbet depth 3-4mm (photo + backing board), or 6mm if there is glass or acrylic.
- Rabbet overlap 4-6mm on each edge: enough to hold the picture, little enough
  that it does not eat the image.
- Aperture = picture size **minus** twice the overlap. So a 6x4 inch photo
  (152 x 102mm) with 5mm overlap gives a 142 x 92mm aperture.
- Border width 12-25mm. Under 10mm looks flimsy and prints weak.
- Front face 6-10mm thick behind the rabbet.

Print it face-down on the bed: the front is the show surface and wants the
smooth first layer.

For a stand, add a wedge leg at 15-20 degrees from vertical, or a keyhole slot
on the back for wall hanging.
