**Call for it, do not build it by hand.** `magnetPocket(6, 3)` cuts the pocket for
a 6x3 disc at the press fit below. The numbers here are the ones it uses — see
#parts.


Common disc magnets (Ø × thick): **6×2, 6×3, 8×3, 10×2, 10×3, 12×3, 15×3, 20×3**.
Cubes: 5 and 10. Default assumption when unstated: **Ø8 × 3**.

Pocket that press-fits: Ø **+0.25**, depth **+0.2**.

The trick worth using — the **captive cover**: make the pocket a blind hole
from the INSIDE/back of the wall, leaving **0.6mm** of solid material between
magnet and the mating face. The magnet is sealed in with zero glue, can't
scratch anything, and 0.6mm costs almost no pull strength. Pause-and-insert
mid-print, or leave the back open and press it in after.

Pairs must attract: two parts that snap together need opposite poles facing —
note it in a comment; the printer can't check polarity.

Strength intuition: an 8×3 N35 disc holds ~1kg pull straight off steel, far
less in shear — for a shear load (fridge hook), use two magnets or add a lip
that takes the weight and let the magnet only hold it closed.

Heat kills them: never place a pocket where an insert will be heat-set nearby,
and don't design for >80°C service.

You have every dimension you need — build it. State the magnet size you assumed.
