title: Brackets and wall mounts
match: bracket, mount, wall mount, wall-mount, hanger, hook, shelf, l-bracket, angle bracket
---

- **Screw clearance**: M3 -> 3.4mm hole, M4 -> 4.5mm, M5 -> 5.5mm. A hole cut to
  the nominal bolt diameter will not pass the bolt.
- **Countersink** for a flat head: 90 degree cone, top diameter 2x the shank.
  For a socket-head cap screw use a counterbore instead: M3 -> 6.5mm dia x 3.5 deep.
- **Wall anchors**: leave 8mm around each hole so the plastic does not split.
- **Load direction**: a bracket carrying weight wants a gusset (a triangular rib)
  in the inside corner. Without it the corner is the layer line and it snaps
  there. Gusset thickness = wall thickness, running the full depth.
- Minimum wall 3mm for anything structural. 2.4mm is cosmetic only.
- Two screws minimum, spaced as far apart as the plate allows — one screw is a
  hinge, not a mount.

Print brackets so the load runs **along** layers, not across them: an L-bracket
is strongest printed lying on its side, not standing up.
