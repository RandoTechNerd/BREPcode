Face dimensions (the printed-bracket side). Columns: **face square · mounting
holes (square pattern, centre-to-centre) · screw · pilot boss Ø · shaft Ø**:

- NEMA 14: 35.2 · 26.0 · M3 · Ø22 · 5
- **NEMA 17** (the printer default): 42.3 · **31.0** · M3 · Ø22 · 5 (D-flat)
- NEMA 23: 56.4 · 47.14 · M5 (Ø5.5 clearance) · Ø38.1 · 6.35

Printed mount rules:

- Boss pocket: pilot Ø **+0.4** (NEMA17 → Ø22.4), 2.2 deep — locates the motor
  so the four screws only clamp.
- Screw holes Ø3.4 (M3) on the 31.0 square; screws reach 4-5mm into the motor
  body, so plate thickness + washer must leave that.
- Shaft clearance hole ≥ Ø8 behind a NEMA17 boss (Ø25 if the belt pulley's
  grub screw must pass through).
- Body lengths vary (NEMA17: 34 / 40 / 48 common) — never box the back in
  without asking or leaving it open; motors run warm, leave one face free.
- Slot two of the four holes 3.4 × 5 when the design also tensions a belt.

You have every dimension you need — build it. NEMA 17 unless told otherwise.
