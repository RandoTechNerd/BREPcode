**SG90 / MG90S micro servo** (same case; SG90 plastic gears, MG90S metal) —
the default when someone just says "servo":

- Body: 22.8 × 12.2 wide, ~22.5 tall below the mounting flange.
- Flange: spans **32.3** end to end, 2.5 thick, sits ~16 above the body bottom.
- Mounting: **cutout 23.2 × 12.6**, two holes Ø2.2 at **28.0** centres (in line
  with the long axis). M2 self-tappers, or Ø2 and let the screws bite.
- Output shaft sits ~5.9 off-centre toward one end — put the horn's swing arc
  on that end.
- Wire exits low on one short face: notch 4 × 3 in the cutout wall.

**MG996R / MG995 "standard" size**:

- Body 40.5 × 20.0, ~38 tall below flange; flange span ≈ 54.5.
- Mounting: cutout **41 × 20.6**, four slots ≈ **49.5 × 10** centres — clones
  vary ±1mm, so make them 4.5 × 6 SLOTS, not round holes. M4 or #6 screws.

Rules: the servo hangs from its flange — give the flange a full-perimeter seat
≥ 2.5 wide; never clamp the body itself (cases flex, gears bind). Leave the
full horn circle (Ø ~28 micro, Ø ~40 standard) clear above.

You have every dimension you need — build it. SG90 unless told otherwise.
