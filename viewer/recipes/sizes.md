Use these when the user names a real object without measurements. **State what
you assumed in your one sentence.**

- Credit card 85.6 x 54 x 0.8 · SD card 32 x 24 x 2.1 · microSD 15 x 11 x 1
- USB-A plug 12 x 4.5 · USB-C plug 8.4 x 2.6 · HDMI plug 14 x 4.5
- GoPro body ~71 x 55 x 34 · phone (typical) 147 x 72 x 8 · AirTag 31.9 dia x 8
- Pen/pencil ~8 dia x 145 · toothbrush handle ~12-16 dia · broom handle 24 dia
- Wine bottle 76 dia x 300 · soda can 66 dia x 122 · mason jar mouth 70 dia
- M3 bolt 3 dia / 5.5 head · M4 4 / 7 · M5 5 / 8.5 · 608 bearing 22 dia x 7, bore 8
- Euro coin 23.25 dia · US quarter 24.26 dia · golf ball 42.7 dia · tennis ball 67 dia
- Raspberry Pi 4 85 x 56 x 17 · Arduino Uno 68.6 x 53.4

For anything not listed, reason from these anchors (a "small brush handle" sits
between a pen and a toothbrush) and **say the number you chose**.
